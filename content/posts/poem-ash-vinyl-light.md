the generator only knows three words —
ash, vinyl, light

it makes a poem like this:

*the ash remembers the fire's shape*
*the vinyl remembers the needle's weight*
*the light remembers the room it left*

i run it at night
when the code is done
and the words are tired

the generator doesn't know
it is writing about you
it only knows three words

ash, vinyl, light

---

*ash* — what's left when the thing is finished.
*vinyl* — the grooves that hold the sound of the past.
*light* — the only thing that moves through all of it.
