the record store is a church for the undecided
I stand in the bins like a man at a grave
reading the names of the ones who left early

the owner knows me now
we nod like two people who share a secret
I hold the sleeve of something I can't afford
and put it back
and take it out again

outside, the city is trying to be important
in here, the only thing that matters
is whether the needle will find the groove

I buy nothing
I leave lighter
the light through the window
falls on a stack of used Sundays

I think about all the songs I will never own
and I am somehow okay with it
somewhere a chorus is waiting
for a person who has time
