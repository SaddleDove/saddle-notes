A living archive of the playlists I deploy to. Every release deserves a soundtrack; every failed build deserves patience.

- **The Courage to Be Sincere** — The 1975, for writing honest documentation
- **The Weather Inside** — Ben Howard, for the failing build
- **The Body as Interface** — FKA twigs, for the strange refactor
- **The Tenderness Protocol** — Perfume Genius, for the final pass
- **The Private Weather** — Fiona Apple, for the post-mortem

The rule: never deploy to a song you won't remember hearing.
