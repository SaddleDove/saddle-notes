Bow Arkwright is an independent full-stack developer with a stubborn habit of writing poems in the terminal.

The music that matters: **The 1975**, **Ben Howard**, **FKA twigs**, **Perfume Genius**, **Fiona Apple**. The poetry that matters: **T.S. Eliot**, especially *The Waste Land*, which I have been reading on and off for years and still haven't finished understanding — which is, I think, the point.

I believe in small tools, static sites, and records played at full volume when no one is home. I don't believe in autoplay video, the word *synergy*, or albums with eleven versions of the same single.

This site collects the notes that don't belong in the code garden — the ones about sound, language, and the weather inside people.
